How CivicFix Works

1. Report

Citizens spot an issue, take a photo, add location, and submit a complaint easily.

2. Assign

Admins verify the issue and assign it to the correct department and officer.

3. Resolve

Officers fix the issue, upload proof, and citizens get notified immediately.
